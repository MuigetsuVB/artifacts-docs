Your characters have 8 skills that can gain XP and reach up to level 50. They allow you to gather resources and craft items.

| Skill | Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Woodcutting** | Gathering, Crafting | Harvesting wood from trees and crafting planks |
| **Mining** | Gathering, Crafting | Extracting ores from rocks and crafting ingots. |
| **Fishing** | Gathering | Catching fish from water sources. |
| **Alchemy** | Gathering, Crafting | Collecting plants and crafting potions. |
| **Weaponcrafting** | Crafting | Forging weapons for combat. |
| **Gearcrafting** | Crafting | Creating armor and equipment. |
| **Jewelrycrafting** | Crafting | Crafting rings and amulets. |
| **Cooking** | Crafting | Preparing food for healing. |

## Gathering

Gathered resources are the foundation of the crafting system, as they are essential for almost every crafting recipe in the game.
To gather a resource, your character must be located on a map tile that contains it. For more details on map navigation, see [Maps and Movement](/concepts/maps_and_movement/).

Here are some examples of resources:

<ResourcePreview codes="copper_rocks,birch_tree,salmon_spot,torch_cactus" />


### XP Formula

Gathering XP is calculated with resource level and your skill level. In the
formula, `player_level` means the level of the skill used to gather, rather than
your combat level. `Round` rounds to the nearest integer, with halves rounded up.

```
XP = Round((XP_base + (resource_level / player_level) × 8) × level_penalty × wisdom_bonus)
```


<br/>
**XP Base** (XP_base):
| Resource Level | XP Base |
|---|---|
| < 10 | 5 |
| 10-19 | 10 |
| 20-29 | 13 |
| 30-34 | 16 |
| 35-39 | 20 |
| 40-44 | 28 |
| 45+ | 36 |
<br/>

**Level Penalty** (level_penalty):<br/>
- Up to 10 levels above the resource → 1.0 (no additional penalty)
- More than 10 levels above the resource → 0 (0 XP)


**Wisdom Bonus** (wisdom_bonus):<br/>
- Formula: (1 + wisdom × 0.001)
- Each wisdom point = +0.1% XP

During Season 9, an active [altar favor](/concepts/seasonal_altar/) for this exact
resource node multiplies the rounded XP by 1.5, then rounds again. It cannot
restore XP lost to the level penalty.

## Crafting

Crafting is an essential core mechanic; the vast majority of items in the game—including equipment, jewelry, weapons, potions, and food—are entirely player-crafted. To craft an item, your character must be on a map tile with a workshop for the matching skill. For more information about maps, see [Maps and Movement](/concepts/maps_and_movement/).

### XP Formula

Crafting XP is calculated from the item level, your crafting skill level, and the
total number of material units consumed by one craft:

`player_level` means your level in the recipe's skill, and `item_level` is the
recipe's required skill level (`craft.level`). This Season 9 formula weights
recipes by their material cost to make crafting progression smoother.

```
XP = Round((XP_base + (item_level / player_level) × coefficient) × crafting_multiplier × level_penalty × wisdom_bonus)

```


<br/>
**XP Base** (XP_base) &  **Coefficient** (coefficient):
| Item Level | XP Base | Coefficient |
|---|---|---|
| < 5 | 50 | 25 |
| 5-9 | 100 | 30 |
| 10-14 | 200 | 35 |
| 15-19 | 325 | 40 |
| 20-24 | 450 | 45 |
| 25-29 | 550 | 50 |
| 30-34 | 650 | 55 |
| 35-39 | 750 | 60 |
| 40-44 | 850 | 65 |
| 45+ | 1000 | 70 |
<br/>

**Crafting Multiplier** (crafting_multiplier):<br/>

For Cooking, Alchemy, Weaponcrafting, Gearcrafting, and Jewelrycrafting:

```
ingredient_count = Sum(quantity of each material in the recipe)
crafting_multiplier = Clamp(0.2832 + 0.2171 × √Max(1, ingredient_count), 0.50, 1.55)
```

`ingredient_count` is the total quantity of materials consumed by one craft,
not the number of distinct material types. For example, a recipe requiring 10
bars and 5 pieces of wood has an `ingredient_count` of 15. The result quantity
of the recipe does not change this value.

The square root makes expensive recipes grant more XP without allowing very
large recipes to scale linearly. The multiplier is limited to a minimum of 0.50
and a maximum of 1.55.

| Material units | Crafting multiplier |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.50 |
| 3 | 0.66 |
| 6 | 0.82 |
| 10 | 0.97 |
| 15 | 1.12 |
| 20 | 1.25 |
| 25 | 1.37 |
| 30 | 1.47 |

Mining and Woodcutting refining recipes use a fixed crafting multiplier of
`0.1` instead of the ingredient-based formula.

For example, at skill level 20 with a level 20 recipe and no wisdom, the base
is `450 + (20 / 20) × 45 = 495` XP:

| Material units per craft | XP per craft |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 248 |
| 3 | 326 |
| 20 | 621 |
| 30 | 729 |

XP is rounded per craft, then summed across the batch. A recipe's output
quantity does not multiply its XP. With 100 wisdom, the 20-material example
grants 683 XP per craft.


**Level Penalty** (level_penalty):<br/>
- Up to 10 levels above the recipe → 1.0 (no additional penalty)
- More than 10 levels above item → 0 (0 XP)


**Wisdom Bonus** (wisdom_bonus):<br/>
- Formula: (1 + wisdom × 0.001)
- Each wisdom point = +0.1% XP

### Gathering and Crafting Effect Keywords

Equipment uses separate effect codes for gathering and crafting cooldowns:

| Skill | Gathering effect | Crafting effect |
|---|---|---|
| Mining | `mining_gathering` | `mining_crafting` |
| Woodcutting | `woodcutting_gathering` | `woodcutting_crafting` |
| Fishing | `fishing_gathering` | — |
| Alchemy | `alchemy_gathering` | `alchemy_crafting` |
| Weaponcrafting | — | `weaponcrafting_crafting` |
| Gearcrafting | — | `gearcrafting_crafting` |
| Jewelrycrafting | — | `jewelrycrafting_crafting` |
| Cooking | — | `cooking_crafting` |

A value of `-20` means a 20% cooldown reduction. Bonuses for the same skill
and action add together. Gathering bonuses only affect gathering; crafting
bonuses only affect crafting. These effects change cooldowns, not XP per action.

For crafting, the combined reduction is capped at 100% and applies to the whole
batch before rounding, with a minimum cooldown of 1 second. For example, 10
crafts at the recipe's level take 50 seconds before bonuses; a `-25` crafting
effect reduces that to 38 seconds after rounding.

:::note[API migration]
Bare skill codes such as `mining`, `woodcutting`, `fishing`, and `alchemy` no
longer grant cooldown reductions. Use the action-specific effect codes above.
Skill identifiers in recipes, resources, workshops, and character levels stay
the same: for example, `craft.skill` is still `alchemy`.
:::

### Craft Action

To craft an item at a workshop, use the following endpoint. You must have the required skill level and materials in your inventory:

**Endpoint:** `POST /my/{name}/action/crafting`

<ApiSdkTabs apiUrl="https://api.artifactsmmo.com/docs/#/operations/action_crafting_my__name__action_crafting_post">

|---|---|
| `code` | The item code to craft. |
| `quantity` | Number of items to craft (optional, default 1). |
